Experience

From medical imaging to production vehicles

Nov 2020 – Present

Auburn Hills, USA · Querétaro, MX

R&D Machine Learning Engineer, Senior Staff

Continental Automotive

Owned multimodal perception models end to end, from framing a high-level objective as an ML task through state-of-the-art research, architecture, losses, training, labeling, evaluation, and deployment in production vehicles.

  • Multimodal 3D perception. Designed and built 2D/3D detection, tracking, and segmentation models, including 3D models on camera images and LiDAR point clouds. Aligned radar, LiDAR, camera, and odometry streams across coordinate frames and sampling rates (synchronization, filtering) so real-time inference gets stable inputs.
  • Deployment in production vehicles. Took models from PyTorch to real-time C++ on an automotive accelerator under strict latency budgets, using knowledge distillation, quantization-aware training, and post-training quantization; the quantized model kept 99% similarity to its floating-point reference. Reshaped architectures so weights and intermediate feature maps fit in on-chip memory (L1/L2/L3) instead of spilling to DDR. PyTorch · ONNX · C++ · ViT · DINOv3
  • Automated labeling strategy. Designed a fully automated labeling pipeline for several terabytes of raw driving recordings, with no costly manual annotation. Iterated for label quality and hard-case coverage, raising reliably labeled frames by 14 percentage points and recovering most of the hardest scenarios. Ran 4–5 recordings concurrently as multi-model GPU processes sized to fit GPU memory, cutting each full-dataset relabel from ~4 days to under 1.
  • Synthetic data for generalization. Found a performance gap on unseen scenarios through held-out cross-validation and built a Blender (Python) synthetic-data pipeline that closed 50% of it without regressing on seen scenarios. Extended it into simulation-based stress tests for rare, safety-critical cases before on-road deployment.
  • 6× faster training. Profiled training end to end and removed bottlenecks in storage I/O, CPU preprocessing, host-to-GPU transfer, and GPU compute; redesigned data loading (formats, sharding, prefetching). Training ran 6× faster at ~90% average GPU utilization, shortening each model iteration. PyTorch · CUDA · NumPy · OpenCV · MLflow
  • Agentic auto-research (PoC). Built an AI-agent loop that ran 100+ training experiments over a weekend, scored on held-out field recordings it never saw. Beyond hyperparameter search, it proposed new loss terms that drove most gains, cutting failing frames (below 90% precision) by 43%.
  • Metrics and evaluation. With no requirements to start from, defined the metrics, from training-time losses to precision, recall, and frame pass rate on field recordings, and aligned them with stakeholders. Built the evaluation pipeline and a centralized visualization platform for KPI tracking, failure inspection, and model comparison. MLflow · Docker · CI/CD
  • Architecture and team leadership (2021–2023). Designed and managed implementation of the solution’s backend architecture and state machines; wrote the design documents and aligned them with existing requirements and algorithm- and system-level tests. Led a team of 4 engineers on one full-stack module, coordinated integration with the teams building other modules, set engineering standards, mentored engineers, and served as Scrum Master.
